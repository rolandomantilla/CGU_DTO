Human variables in this simulation

Skills use task-relevant proficiency (1–5; a missing competency is treated as 0). Experience uses explicit years in role or similar-task counts. Relationships use directed handoffs and collaboration events over a 30-day observation window. All added records are synthetic demonstration data.

Assumptions and auditability

The simulation uses a deterministic bottleneck model grounded in linked staff, skills, assignments, workflows, and systems. This is not a statistical risk forecast, trained AI prediction, or ArcGIS integration. Capacity findings depend on synthetic assumptions and need validation before operational use.
